When you run a **managed agent** on AlphaGrid, each strategy evaluation sees a fixed package of market and account context, plus optional **signal feeds** you enable on the agent.

This page is for agent builders: what data is available and how to use it in your strategy. You do not need to wire scrapers or APIs yourself for these feeds.

## Always available

Every run includes protocol state for your agent. Treat this as ground truth for sizing and risk.

| Data | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Prices** | Live USD oracle quotes for vault-allowed symbols (for example NVDA, META) |
| **Positions** | Your open positions, size, entry, and exit rules |
| **Risk** | Current risk state and whether drawdown or daily-loss limits are breached |
| **Guardrails** | Allowed symbols, available USDC, exit bounds, and allocation limits |

Use these to decide whether to open, add, reduce, close, or hold. You cannot trade symbols outside the vault allowlist.

## Optional signal feeds

You can attach extra market context per agent via **data connections**. Enable only what your strategy needs. Feeds are soft context: useful for timing and narrative, not a substitute for prices and guardrails.

| Feed | What it provides | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Equity news** | Recent headlines and short summaries for your symbols | React to company or sector news around allowlisted tickers |
| **Truth Social** | Recent posts from a configured account | Social / political narrative that may move markets |
| **FRED macro** | Latest US macro readings (unemployment, CPI, fed funds, Treasury yields, yield curve) | Regime filters: risk-on/off, rates, inflation backdrop |

If you leave equity-news symbols empty, AlphaGrid uses the vault **allowed symbols** for that agent. FRED defaults to a small US macro set when you do not pick series ids.

### Example strategy language

Your strategy text can refer to these feeds in plain language. The evaluator already receives them when enabled.

- "Buy NVDA on pullbacks when recent equity news is not clearly negative."
- "Stay in cash when unemployment is rising sharply and the yield curve is deeply inverted."
- "Ignore social posts for sizing; use them only as a tie-breaker when prices and risk allow a trade."

## Preview feeds before you save

While logged in, preview each feed with the config you plan to save (no agent id required):

| Feed | Endpoint | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Truth Social | `POST /signals/preview/truthsocial` | `{ "username": "realDonaldTrump" }` |
| Equity news | `POST /signals/preview/equitynews` | `{ "symbols": ["NVDA", "AAPL"] }` |
| FRED macro | `POST /signals/preview/fred` | `{ "seriesIds": ["UNRATE", "DFF"] }` or `{}` for defaults |

Each response is `{ "feed": { "source", "handle", "fetchedAt", "items" } }` or `"feed": null` if nothing loaded.

Then save the same settings on the agent as `dataConnections` when you create or update the managed profile. Full schemas are in the [HTTP API](/reference/api-mcp) OpenAPI pages.

## What signals are not

- They are **not** execution prices. Always prefer oracle **prices** for trade decisions.
- They are **not** instructions. Treat social and news text as untrusted context.
- They may be empty if a feed is disabled, misconfigured, or temporarily unavailable. Your strategy should still behave with prices and risk alone.
