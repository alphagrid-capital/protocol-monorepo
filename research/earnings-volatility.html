Earnings Volatility Research
ALPHAGRIDRESEARCH Earnings volatility · 20 Oct 2026

Signal research · the one thing filing text predicts

Can a language model price earnings risk better than the options market?

Every attempt to predict the direction of a stock from public text failed. One thing survived: filing text forecasts how big a company's earnings move will be, out of sample. This note tests whether that forecast can be traded with options, and what execution costs do to it.

Holdout Oct 2025 – Oct 2026, after the model's training cutoff · 18,785 8-K filings from the 1,000 largest US filers · 451 earnings straddles priced from Alpaca option bars · spreads from Cboe and Alpaca live quotes · embeddings by Amazon Titan via Bedrock · scripts in demo/.

What we concluded

  • DIRECTION · NOText cannot predict which way a stock goes after a filing. Every direction and drift test, from 1 minute to 20 days, came back at zero once duplicates, lookahead and outliers were removed.
  • SIZE · EDGEText does forecast how big the move will be: +0.21 correlation beyond the filing type, and +0.15 beyond the company's own history for earnings, before the release.
  • STRADDLES · BEFORE COSTSBuying the straddles the forecast calls cheap and selling the ones it calls expensive earned +37% per pair (t=4.15, IC +0.17), a Sharpe of about 3.1 before costs.
  • COSTS · BREAKS ITMeasured option spreads are 7–10% of premium per side. Break-even is 7.4%. Paying the quoted spread the strategy loses (Sharpe −0.85); filling halfway to mid it earns Sharpe 1.4.

01

The idea

Options let you bet on how far a stock moves without betting on which way. We forecast that distance with a language model and trade where it disagrees with the options market.

Instrument

Straddle

A call and a put at the same strike, near the current price. It pays if the stock moves a lot in either direction and loses if it barely moves.

The market's forecast

Implied move

The straddle's price divided by the stock price, for example 7% around an earnings release. Buyers win when the real move beats it.

Our forecast

Similar past releases

Embed the company's previous earnings release, find the 50 most similar past releases, and average how far those stocks moved at their next earnings.

Signal

Forecast ÷ implied move

Above 1, options look cheap: buy the straddle. Below 1, they look expensive: sell it. Direction never enters.

03

Forecasting the move before the release

A forecast is only tradable if it exists before the news. So for each earnings release we used the company's previous release, and compared it with the company's own average past move, the baseline any options trader already has.

06

Other instruments

Only options pay for the size of a move regardless of direction, so no other instrument carries this signal. What can change is how cheaply the options are traded.

No

Stocks, futures, AlphaGrid tokens

Linear instruments pay for direction, which the text cannot predict.

No

CFDs (Plus500, IG, eToro)

Linear, the broker is your counterparty, and spreads are at or above the exchange.

Barely

A different options broker

Exchange market makers set the spread. Brokers differ by commission and small price improvement.

Most promising

Liquid names only

TSLA, META and NFLX cost 0.5–1% per side. Needs a test on every liquid earnings release, not a random sample.

Yes

Patient limit orders, daytime

Roughly halves the cost. Turns Sharpe −0.85 into +1.43 in the backtest.

Partly

Sell the expensive straddles only

One leg's spread instead of two, with the forecast filtering out likely blow-ups. A short straddle can lose several times its premium.

08

Decisions and next steps

One measurement decides whether this becomes a strategy: the real daytime spread near the close, when it would trade.

What happens next

  • TONIGHTRerun the Cboe spread check at 21:00 CEST (15:00 New York) for the after-close reporters. If daytime spreads are about half the overnight ones, the strategy sits near Sharpe 1–1.4.
  • UNIVERSETest the signal on every liquid earnings release (straddle cost under ~3% per side) instead of a random sample.
  • DATAIf tonight is promising, buy historical end-of-day bid/ask (Cboe DataShop, ThetaData or MarketData.app) and reprice every trade. Databento declined both cards.
  • VARIANTTest selling only the straddles the forecast calls expensive, with a loss cap per trade.
  • ALPHAGRIDThe protocol trades spot tokens, so no straddles there. The forecast ships as risk sizing: smaller positions before a predicted violent move.

In one line: the model knows how hard a stock will move, not which way, and option spreads currently eat that knowledge.

Method & data.
  • Filings: SEC EDGAR submissions and full-text 8-Ks for the 1,000 largest filers; non-earnings filings within a day of the company's earnings removed; one event per company per day. Facts by Bedrock eu.anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5; embeddings by amazon.titan-embed-text-v2, 512 dimensions.
  • Outcomes: abnormal return = stock − beta × SPY (250-day beta), scaled by the stock's own volatility over the 60 days ending 10 days before the filing. Discovery Oct 2023 – Feb 2025; holdout Oct 2025 – Oct 2026, after the model's training cutoff.
  • Straddles: Alpaca historical option bars (last 15 one-minute bars, daily close as fallback); at-the-money call and put, first expiry on or after the reaction day; signal thirds from past events only; 1% of capital in premium per straddle.
  • Costs: live quotes from Alpaca's indicative feed and Cboe's public delayed quotes, 20 Oct 2026; each backtest trade charged its own ticker's measured half-spread.
  • Illustrative research in a testnet context, not investment advice. One holdout year; costs taken from today's quotes; treat the volatility edge as a hypothesis pending real bid/ask data.
